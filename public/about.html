About A Word in Season

A companion for preachers and church speakers: hand-picked Bible verses for the occasions a church family celebrates and mourns together, with an opening prayer, talking points and a closing blessing for each — in English, Hindi and Telugu. Start from any of the 17 occasions, for example weddings, baptisms or funerals.

Dedication

This site was built for Bro. Raj Timothy of Smyrna House of Fellowship, who brings a word from Scripture to the church family's celebrations and sorrows. May it help that word always come in season.

Podium mode

Press Present on any verse, or Present all on an occasion, to show the verses full-screen in large type — for reading from a phone at the pulpit or for a projector. Tap the left or right side of the screen, swipe, or use the arrow keys (most presentation clickers work too). B blanks the screen, +/− change the text size and Esc closes. The screen is kept awake while presenting.

Use it offline

Install the site on your phone (Android: the Install button in the header; iPhone: Share → Add to Home Screen). It then opens from its own icon and works without an internet connection.
