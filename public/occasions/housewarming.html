Speaker's kit

Opening prayer

Lord, we dedicate this home to You. Let it be a place of peace, prayer and welcome. Bless every room with Your presence, every meal with gratitude and every conversation with kindness. Guard those who live here in their going out and coming in, from this time forth and for evermore. In Jesus' name, Amen.

Talking points

  1. The Lord builds the house

    Walls and a roof make a house; God's presence makes a home.

    Psalm 127:1
  2. As for me and my house

    The most important decision for a home is whom it will serve.

    Joshua 24:15
  3. Filled with precious things

    Wisdom and understanding fill a home with treasures money cannot buy.

    Proverbs 24:3-4
