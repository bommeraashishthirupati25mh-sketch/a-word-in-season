Speaker's kit

Opening prayer

Lord of the harvest, we thank You for calling Your servant into ministry. Anoint them with Your Holy Spirit. Give them a shepherd's heart, a faithful tongue and a humble spirit. Protect their family, strengthen them in weariness and let many come to know You through their service. In Jesus' name, Amen.

Talking points

  1. Here am I, send me

    Ministry begins with a willing heart answering God's call.

    Isaiah 6:8
  2. Shepherd the flock

    Leadership in the church is care, not control.

    1 Peter 5:2-4
  3. Preach the word

    In season and out of season, the task is to be faithful to the Word.

    2 Timothy 4:2
