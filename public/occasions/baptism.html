Speaker's kit

Opening prayer

Lord Jesus, today we rejoice as Your child follows You in the waters of baptism. As they are buried with You and raised to walk in newness of life, fill them with Your Holy Spirit. Keep them faithful, make them fruitful, and let this day be remembered as a new beginning. In Your name we pray, Amen.

Talking points

  1. Obedience to Christ

    Baptism is not our idea — it is the command of Jesus to all who follow Him.

    Matthew 28:19-20
  2. Buried and raised

    Going down into the water pictures death to the old life; coming up pictures new life in Christ.

    Romans 6:4
  3. A new creation

    The old has gone; the new has come.

    2 Corinthians 5:17
